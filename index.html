Our Story

As told by Erie

December 2018

It was December 2018, before the pandemic. I was scrolling through Facebook while I was at NAIA when I unexpectedly came across her profile. I remember thinking how beautiful she was, so I searched for her on Facebook and eventually sent her a friend request.

I wanted to get to know her, so with the help of a friend, I found a way to reach out to her. At that time, I wasn't using a smartphone yet, only a keypad phone, so communicating wasn't as easy as it is today. I even tried giving her a diary notebook from PMA, hoping it would catch her attention and give us a chance to get to know each other.

But things didn't move forward. Perhaps it simply wasn't the right time. We were young and still had a lot to learn about ourselves. Looking back, I believe God allowed our paths to cross early because we still needed time to grow individually before we could grow together.

Paths crossing again

After that, I simply continued with my work and studies at the Philippine Military Academy. As time passed, our paths crossed again through communication, and eventually, we started talking, texting, and having phone calls. It was a pleasant surprise to reconnect with her. Hahaha!

Our communication wasn't very frequent because of the nature of my work and training at PMA. We could only call each other during authorized free time, whenever we were permitted to use our phones. Nevertheless, we made the most of the little time we had to communicate and get to know each other better.

During that time, we still hadn't met in person. My responsibilities at the academy made it difficult for us to see each other.

December 23, 2019 · Zamboanga City

Then came December 22, 2019. It was finally time for our R&R and Yuletide break. She was attending CEAP in Zamboanga City, and fortunately, there was a C-130 flight heading there. The timing couldn't have been more perfect. I truly believe God had a hand in bringing everything together.

I decided not to tell her that I was already on break because I wanted to surprise her. Finally, on December 23, 2019, we met face to face for the very first time in Zamboanga City.

I hitched a ride on the transportation provided by the school, and although we had less than eight hours together, it was a moment worth remembering.

We saw each other again around Christmas, but our time together was still brief.

December 28, 2019 · Manawan, Jose Dalman

On December 26, I invited her to my younger sibling's debut celebration, which was scheduled for December 28 in Manawan, Jose Dalman.

Little did she know, I already had something else in mind. Hahaha! I was planning to ask her to officially be my girlfriend. After all that time getting to know each other and finally meeting in person, I thought it was time to take that next step.

2020 – 2021 · Apart

After we officially became a couple, I had to return to the Philippine Military Academy on January 4, 2020. It was difficult to say goodbye, especially after finally having the chance to be together in person. Little did we know that a much bigger challenge was about to come. The COVID-19 pandemic happened, and all our plans of seeing each other and going home throughout 2020 were suddenly put on hold.

After 17 months at PMA, I finally graduated and was able to come home in May 2021. I thought that would mean things would become easier for us, but it was only another chapter of our journey.

Choosing each other

As time went by, we got to know each other on a much deeper level. We discovered more about each other's personalities, beliefs, and perspectives. And as we learned more about one another, we also encountered more challenges. We didn't always share the same views, and there were plenty of times when our differences led to disagreements. There were even moments when we almost went our separate ways.

But through every difficult season, we learned that love wasn't just about being happy together. It was also about understanding, forgiving, compromising, and choosing each other even when things weren't easy.

Above all, we held on to our faith and kept God at the center of our relationship. We knew that we couldn't figure everything out on our own, so we continued to seek His guidance. And somehow, through His grace, we found our way through every challenge.

Despite all the disagreements and difficult days, I continued choosing her. No matter how stressful life became, she was always there. Even during our hardest days, she somehow knew how to bring happiness into my life and lighten my mood. Before I knew it, we would find ourselves laughing again, as if the weight of the day had suddenly become a little lighter.

The woman I prayed for

One thing I love about us is how we can be completely crazy together. We get to do silly things, tease each other, joke around, and play like kids wherever we are. We don't have to worry about looking too childish or immature because we never judge each other for simply being ourselves. We just enjoy being crazy together. SABAY SA TAMA! Hahaha!

Beyond all the fun and laughter, she inspires me to become a better person. When I think about my future, she's the person I see myself building a life with. She values communication and is very straightforward, which has taught me the importance of being open with each other.

She is my home, the person I find comfort in no matter what life brings. She's also the one who takes care of me whenever I get sick, making sure I'm okay even in the simplest ways.

And perhaps one of the most meaningful things I can say about her is that she is the woman I prayed for. I may not have known exactly when or how God would answer that prayer, but looking at where we are today, I am grateful that He allowed our paths to cross and gave us the chance to grow together.

“She became more than the woman I fell in love with. She became my best friend, my safe place, my greatest inspiration, and the person I want to experience all of life's adventures with.” Erie
